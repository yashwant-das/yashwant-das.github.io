# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Tech recruiters, engineering hiring managers and peer test architects, usually checking the site alongside Yashwant's resume or LinkedIn, on desktop or phone. Secondary: engineers exploring his open-source AI testing work.

## Product Purpose

A calm personal site, deliberately not a resume. In under a minute it shows who Yashwant Das is (a test automation architect in Bengaluru), who he has worked with, what he works with, and what he is building, and it makes him easy to contact.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone:** measured, confident and engineering-minded. Concrete mechanics over AI hype.
- **Visual language:** quiet and typographic. Neutral greys, a serif display face, hairline grids and monochrome logos, with one accent reserved for focus. Dark by default, with light held to the same standard.

## Product Principles

1. A person, not a resume: show identity, range and current work, and leave dates and job history to the resume.
2. Typography and whitespace carry the page; the interface recedes.
3. Every personal fact comes from `data/content.json`.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes: keyboard access, visible focus, a skip link, sensible heading order and reduced-motion support.
